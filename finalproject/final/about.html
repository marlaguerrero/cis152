About Us

Pacific Trails Resort is a serene coastal destination where the beauty of nature meets the comfort of modern luxury. Right along the California coastline, our resort offers guests a unique opportunity to escape the noise of everyday life and reconnect with the peaceful rhythms of the ocean. From the moment you arrive, you are welcomed by breathtaking panoramic views, fresh coastal air, and a calming atmosphere designed to help you relax and recharge.

At Pacific Trails Resort, we specialize in creating memorable experiences through our thoughtfully designed accommodations and personalized services. Our private yurts provide a perfect blend of rustic charm and contemporary comfort, allowing guests to immerse themselves in nature without sacrificing convenience. Each stay includes carefully curated amenities and access to scenic trails, guided tours, and tranquil spaces where you can unwind at your own pace.

Whether you spend your day exploring the coastline, enjoying a quiet sunset, or simply relaxing in our yurts, every moment is designed to inspire a sense of peace and connection. Our commitment to sustainability, hospitality, and exceptional guest experiences ensures that your stay is not only enjoyable, but truly unforgettable. At Pacific Trails Resort, we invite you to slow down, breathe deeply, and experience the natural beauty of the coast like never before.
